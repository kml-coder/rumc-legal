Community guidelines

  1. Be kind. Posts are about a nice moment, not about rating or mocking anyone.
  2. No full names, @handles, phone numbers, room numbers, or class schedules of the person you saw.
  3. Never follow, wait for, or track someone down. If they don't respond, let it go.
  4. No sexual comments, threats, or harassment. Ever.
  5. Only send an &UR if you really think it was you.
  6. Report anything that feels unsafe.

There is no tolerance for objectionable content or abusive users. We review reports and remove content and accounts that break these rules.

Support

Contact us

  • In the app: open the me tab → send us feedback. Every message reaches the team.
  • On the web: open a support request.

Report someone or something

Tap ··· on a post, long-press a comment, or tap ··· on an &UR request or a chat, then choose report. Reports are private. When several people report the same thing, it goes straight to a person for review.

Block someone

Tap ··· on their &UR request or in your chat with them and choose block. They can't message you or send you requests, and they aren't notified.

Delete your account

me tab → scroll down → delete account. This permanently deletes your profile, photo, likes, requests and messages, and hides your posts and comments.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

RU Missed Connections ("the app", "we") is an independent app for college students aged 18 and older. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have.

What we collect

  • Account: your school email address and the name on your Google account, used to sign you in and confirm you're a student.
  • Profile: the name, photo and bio you add. Only people you send an &UR request to, and people you match with, can see your profile.
  • Posts and comments: the text you write. They are shown to other signed-in students without your name or any identifier.
  • &UR requests and messages: your note to a poster, and messages in chats you've matched into. Only the people in that conversation can read them.
  • Likes, reports, blocks and feedback you send.
  • Birthday: used once to check you're 18 or older. We do not store it.

We don't use advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs, we don't track you across other apps or websites, and we don't sell your data.

How it's used

  • To run the app: show posts, deliver requests and messages, and keep your profile.
  • To keep people safe: enforce the community guidelines, block slurs, threats and phone numbers or emails in public posts, and review reports.
  • To answer feedback and support requests.

Who processes it

  • Supabase hosts our database, file storage and sign-in (servers in the United States).
  • Google handles "Sign in with Google" for school accounts. We never see your password.
  • Apple distributes the app.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account exists. Posts are shown in the feed for the drop they belong to and can be found later by search. Reports may be kept after an account is deleted so moderators can review safety issues, without linking them to the deleted account.

Your choices

  • Edit your name, bio and photo in the me tab.
  • Block people and report content from within the app.
  • Delete your account any time in the me tab. Your profile, photo, likes, requests and messages are deleted, and your posts and comments are hidden.

Age

The app is only for people 18 and older. If we learn an account belongs to someone younger, we delete it.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections. Access is limited by database rules so other users can never see who wrote a post or read conversations they aren't part of.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above. Questions: use send us feedback in the app or contact us here.
